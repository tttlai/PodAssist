# PodAssist — Cold DM Campaign Playbook

Everything you need to launch a personalized outreach campaign to 50+ podcast hosts this week.

---

## Part 1 — Cold DM Templates

### Template A: The "Specific Episode" Hook (highest conversion)

> **Subject:** Loved your episode with [guest name]
>
> Hey [First name],
>
> Caught your episode with [guest] last week — especially when you pushed on [specific moment / quote / topic]. That follow-up was gold.
>
> Quick context: I'm building **PodAssist** — an AI co-host that listens live and flags shaky claims, suggests follow-ups, and marks viral clip moments as they happen. Basically, the tool I wish you'd had when [reference something from that episode].
>
> Early access is free — no pitch, just 30 seconds if you're curious: **[your-link.com]**
>
> Either way, big fan of the show.
>
> — [Your name]

**Why it works:** Proves you listened. Ties tool value to their actual work. No pushy ask.

---

### Template B: The "Problem First" Hook

> Hey [First name],
>
> Weird question: ever end an interview and realize you missed the *perfect* follow-up? Or found out post-publish that a guest's stat was off?
>
> Been there too. Built something for it — **PodAssist** listens during your recording and quietly flags follow-ups + shaky claims in real time.
>
> Early access is free. 30 seconds here: **[your-link.com]**
>
> Would genuinely love your brain on it — you interview a *lot* of people and I trust your instinct.
>
> — [Your name]

**Why it works:** Leads with empathy. Frames them as expert, not customer.

---

### Template C: The Fact-Check Angle (for journalists / news-style hosts)

> Hey [First name],
>
> Your [episode name] episode was one of the sharpest fact-checking reads I've heard all month. The [specific call-out] was exactly the kind of thing most hosts let slide.
>
> Building a tool — **PodAssist** — that flags shaky claims *during* the interview, quietly on-screen. Thought you'd appreciate the angle since you already do this manually.
>
> Free early access, 30s to check: **[your-link.com]**
>
> — [Your name]

**Why it works:** Best angle for journalism-leaning hosts. Mirrors their existing craft.

---

### Template D: The Short Twitter/X DM (50 chars max)

> Hey [name] — built a tool that flags shaky claims & follow-ups *live* during recording. Early access free, thought you'd appreciate it given your interviewing style: [link]

**Why it works:** Mobile-friendly. Low friction. Fits Twitter's format.

## Part 2 — Personalization Framework

**Every DM must reference ONE of these (takes 2 min per host):**

| Signal | Where to find it |
|--------|------------------|
| A specific guest they had | Latest 3 episodes on their feed |
| A moment that landed | Their most-clipped YouTube short / TikTok |
| A recurring theme in the show | Show description / their Twitter bio |
| An article they wrote / tweet that went off | Their Twitter/LinkedIn |
| Their show's positioning/tagline | Listen Notes / Podchaser bio |

**Rule**: If you can't cite something specific in 20 seconds, skip them.

## Part 4 — How to Find the Remaining 20+ Yourself

Use these tools to scale from 30 → 50+ targets:

### Listen Notes (best tool)
1. Go to [listennotes.com](https://listennotes.com)
2. Search: *"interview"* or *"conversations"* + vertical (e.g., *"fitness interview podcast"*)
3. **Filter**: "Individual" publisher (avoids networks like NPR/Wondery)
4. **Filter**: Episode count 10–100 (active but not massive)
5. Copy host name + email from show page

### Podchaser
1. Go to [podchaser.com](https://podchaser.com)
2. Browse by category → filter "Independent" badge
3. Each show has contact/social links for the host

### Twitter/X Search
1. Search: `podcast host ("just published" OR "new episode")`
2. Filter: "People" → filter by "Follower count: 500–10,000"
3. These hosts almost always run the show themselves

### Spotify "More Like This"
1. Pick 3 shows from Tier 1 above
2. Look at Spotify's "fans also listened to"
3. 10+ new targets per seed show

### X/Twitter hashtags
- `#podcasting`, `#podcasthost`, `#indiepodcaster`
- DM people actively tweeting about their shows

**Target vertical mix:**
- 15 tech/SaaS interview shows
- 10 business/entrepreneurship
- 10 creator economy
- 8 journalism/news interviewing
- 7 niche (fitness, travel, culture, etc.)

## Part 5 — Subject Line Variations

For email DMs (if you have their email):

- `Loved your [guest name] episode`
- `Quick question from a fellow podcast nerd`
- `Thought you'd find this interesting`
- `[Podcast name] → this tool should exist`
- `Built something you might actually use`

**Don't use:**
- ❌ "Revolutionary AI podcast tool"
- ❌ "Increase your podcast by 300%"
- ❌ Anything that reads like SaaS spam

---

## Part 6 — Follow-up Sequence

**Send only ONE follow-up.** Respect their inbox.

### Follow-up DM (send 5-7 days after no reply)

> Hey [name] — totally get if timing's off. If PodAssist is interesting, the link's still here: **[link]**
>
> If not, no worries — still loving the show. [Reference last episode you listened to].
>
> — [Your name]

**After that: move on.** No 3rd or 4th follow-up. Desperation converts negatively.

|------|------|----------|--------|----------|-----------|----------|------------|-------|

**Metrics to watch:**
- **Send → reply rate** (aim for 20%+ if personalization is good)
- **Reply → signup rate** (aim for 50%+ if the DM is honest)
- **Signup → feedback quality** (the whole point of validation)

---

## Part 8 — Weekly Cadence

**Monday**: Research 15 new hosts (1 hour)
**Tue–Thu**: Send 5 DMs/day with fresh personalization
**Friday**: Follow up with previous week's non-responders
**Sunday**: Review replies, update spreadsheet, iterate templates

**Total: 20-25 DMs/week, sustainable long-term.**

---

## Part 9 — Red Flags to Avoid

- ❌ **Don't use mail-merge tools.** Hosts spot them instantly.
- ❌ **Don't pitch in the first line.** Hook with a reference first.
- ❌ **Don't send from `hello@podassist.io`** — send from your personal email/account. Hosts reply to humans, not brands.
- ❌ **Don't ask for calls.** Just the waitlist link. Calls come later if they're interested.
- ❌ **Don't bulk-tag them on social.** Private DM > public tag.
