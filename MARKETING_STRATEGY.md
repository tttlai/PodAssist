# PodAssist — Marketing Strategy

A focused playbook for driving waitlist signups as a solo/indie founder on a low budget. Prioritized by effort-to-impact ratio.

---

## Week 1 — Quick Wins (Free, High Signal)

### 1. Reddit drop (highest ROI)
- Target subreddits: `r/podcasting` (250k members), `r/podcasters`, `r/NewPodcasts`, `r/PodcastSharing`
- **Don't pitch** — write a genuine post:
  > "I missed a great follow-up in my last interview — built a tool that flags them live. Would love feedback from fellow hosts."
- Share the link in comments when asked, not in the post body (Reddit hates direct promo)

### 2. Indie Hackers + Hacker News "Show HN"
- Post as a "validation experiment" — HN respects honesty about pre-product stages
- Title: *"Show HN: PodAssist — AI co-host that listens live (validating demand)"*

### 3. Twitter/X "building in public" thread
- Write a 5-tweet thread: problem → your story → mockup/screenshot → waitlist link
- Tag 2–3 podcast tools (Riverside, Descript) — their audiences overlap perfectly

---

## Week 2–4 — Community Infiltration

### 4. Facebook Groups (goldmine, underrated)
- "Podcast Movement Community" (40k+ members)
- "She Podcasts"
- "Podcasters' Support Group"
- Post a value-add first (tip/insight), mention tool softly in bio/comment

### 5. Podcaster Discord servers
- "Podcasters Hangout"
- "Podchaser Creators"
- Be present, help others, drop link only when contextually relevant

### 6. Direct DM campaign (100 podcasters)
- Find indie interview podcasts via [Listen Notes](https://listennotes.com) or [Podchaser](https://podchaser.com)
- Personal DM template:
  > "Loved your episode with [guest]. Building a tool that would've flagged [specific moment] — 30s to check it out?"
- Expect **10–20% conversion** if personalized

---

## Ongoing — Content Leverage

### 7. YouTube Shorts / TikTok demo
- Mock up the UI reacting to a real podcast clip (use a famous interview moment)
- Hook: *"Watch AI catch a factual error in [famous podcast] in real-time"*
- **This is your viral play**

### 8. SEO blog posts (compound growth)
Target keywords:
- "how to be a better podcast interviewer"
- "AI podcast tools 2026"
- "real-time transcription for podcasters"

Write 3 posts, each ending with a waitlist CTA.

---

## Conversion Boosters (Do These First)

Before any traffic hits, add these to the landing page:

- [ ] **Counter**: "🔥 342 hosts on the waitlist" (social proof)
- [ ] **Referral incentive**: "Skip the queue — refer 3 friends for priority access"
- [ ] **Exit intent popup** with email capture
- [ ] **Twitter/LinkedIn share buttons** on the success toast

---

## Realistic Target Numbers

For a solo founder executing all the above:

| Month | Signups |
|-------|---------|
| 1     | 200–500 |
| 2     | 1,000–2,000 (if one Reddit/HN post pops) |
| 3     | 3,000+ (if demo video goes viral) |

---

## The Honest Truth

**500 engaged podcasters is way more valuable for validation than 5,000 random signups.**

Optimize for getting hosts who:
- Reply to your emails
- Answer calls
- Give detailed feedback
- Would actually pay

Email count is a vanity metric. Engagement is the real signal.

---

## Weekly Execution Checklist

**Every week:**
- [ ] 1 Reddit post in a different subreddit
- [ ] 10 cold DMs to target podcasters
- [ ] 1 Twitter thread or long-form post
- [ ] 3 meaningful comments in Discord/Facebook groups
- [ ] Track waitlist growth + reply rate

**Every month:**
- [ ] 1 blog post published
- [ ] 1 YouTube Short / TikTok posted
- [ ] Review analytics, cut what's not working, double down on what is
